# QuickAid Collective

### Emergency care, owned by the people who deliver it.

QuickAid Collective is a proposed community-powered emergency assistance platform designed to make emergency response easier to coordinate during accidents and medical emergencies.

The platform connects people who need help with nearby participating responders and service providers while exploring a member-owned model where the people delivering the service can have a genuine voice and share in the value they create.

> **QuickAid Collective is a prototype/ideation project and is not a replacement for public emergency services.**

---

## 🚨 The Problem

During accidents and medical emergencies, people often have to manage several things separately:

- Contact emergency services
- Share their location
- Inform family or trusted contacts
- Communicate medical information
- Find nearby assistance
- Decide what immediate action to take

This fragmented process can create unnecessary delays during the first few minutes of an emergency.

At the same time, frontline responders and local emergency-service providers often have limited ownership or influence over the digital platforms that connect them with people who need their services.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

QuickAid Collective brings emergency coordination into one community-powered platform.

The proposed platform focuses on:

- 🆘 Emergency assistance requests
- 📍 Location sharing
- 👨‍👩‍👧 Trusted contact alerts
- ❤️ Digital medical information
- 🚑 Nearby responder coordination
- 📞 Access to emergency services such as 112 and 108
- 🤖 AI-assisted emergency and first-aid guidance

The goal is simple:

**Make emergency assistance easier to access, faster to coordinate, and more community-powered.**
